Simple Banking Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Simple Banking is made by Generations Arising. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the Simple Banking app on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

The short version

Your records belong to you. We do not collect, see, sell, or share any of the information you enter. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The app does not connect to your bank.

What you store in the app

You can enter the names of companies you pay, account numbers, phone numbers, addresses, websites, bill amounts and due dates, deposits, your checking balance, tax categories, and pictures of where a payment cleared. This information is stored:

  • On your device, and
  • In your own private iCloud account, if iCloud is turned on for your Apple Account, so your records stay in sync across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Information in your private iCloud is protected by Apple and is only available to devices signed in to your Apple Account. We, the developer, cannot access it. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple protects iCloud data.

Photos

If you attach a picture to a payment, the app only receives the picture you choose; it can't see the rest of your photo library.

Information we collect

None. The app does not send your information to us or to anyone else. It contains no third-party code for advertising, analytics, or crash reporting.

Backups and reports you create

If you use Export Backup or export the year-end tax report, the app creates a file and saves or shares it wherever you choose. You control that file. Anyone you share it with can read it, so keep it somewhere safe.

Deleting your information

You can delete any bill, deposit, or account in the app. Deleting the app removes its records from that device. To remove the copy in iCloud, delete the records in the app first, or manage iCloud storage in your device's Settings.

Not financial advice

Simple Banking is a personal bill and deposit tracker. It doesn't connect to your bank or move money; amounts are only what you enter. It is not financial or tax advice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
