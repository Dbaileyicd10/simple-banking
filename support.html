Common questions

How do I get started?
Tap the balance at the top of the Ledger and enter what your bank shows. Then tap + to add your bills and deposits. The bottom of the screen shows what's left after bills to pay and deposits not yet cleared.

What happens when I mark a bill paid?
It moves to your payment history and next month's bill is created for you (or every 2 weeks or every year, depending on the account). Changed your mind? Mark it unpaid and the extra copy is removed.

Can I keep a picture of the payment?
Yes. When you mark a bill paid you can attach a picture of where it cleared the bank.

How do I get a year-end report for my tax preparer?
Give each account a tax category, then on the Accounts tab tap More → Tax Report and export it as a spreadsheet file.

How do I see what it looks like with bills in it?
Open the menu (☰) → Add Sample Bills. Everything named "Sample" is made up. Remove it the same way; your own bills aren't touched.

How do my records get to my other devices?
Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and install Simple Banking on each iPhone, iPad, or Mac. Bills and your balance sync on their own.

How do I back up or move my records?
Open the menu (☰) → Export Backup to save a file. To restore, tap Restore from Backup and choose that file. Restoring adds anything missing and skips duplicates.

Is my information private?
Yes. It stays on your devices and in your private iCloud account. We can't see it, and the app never connects to your bank. Read the privacy policy.
